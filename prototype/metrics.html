What the page records

  • That a visit started, whether it came from a shared link, and a rough screen size (small, medium or large).
  • Which answers were picked (state, priorities, project stage), and how long it took to see results.
  • Whether someone opened more detail, used a bill filter, opened a glossary word, copied the link, printed, or started over.

It never records what someone types in a search box, who they are, or where they are. No cookies are used. A reload counts as a new visit. If the browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is sent anywhere. By default nothing is sent at all, and the counts live in your own browser.
